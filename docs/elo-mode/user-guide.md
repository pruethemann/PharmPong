# Elo Mode: guide for players

Elo Mode is the social part of the evening after the tournament: free matches on both tables, teams formed
anew for every match, and a live Elo ranking. The app is in German; the labels below are quoted exactly as they
appear on screen.

## 1. Join in

- Sign in with your PharmaPong account (or register). Without an account you can still watch: tables,
  ranking and results are public while the organizers have the public display on.
- Every table has a QR code. Scanning it with the phone camera opens the table's page. The code only shows
  the table; it does not sign you in or start anything by itself.

## 2. Start a match

1. Tap **"Match erstellen"** on the home page, or **"Hier ein Match starten"** on a free table (or on the
   table's QR page).
2. **Tisch:** pick a free table. Busy and closed tables cannot be chosen.
3. **Teams:** you are already in Team A. Tap **"Spieler:innen wählen"** to add people: search by name, tap
   to add or remove, **"Fertig"** to close the list. Each team has 1 to 4 players. People who are playing
   elsewhere are greyed out with the reason ("spielt an Tisch 2").
4. **Vorschau ("Was steht auf dem Spiel?"):** the average Elo of both teams, the win probability and how much
   Elo each person wins or loses for both outcomes. Switch off **"Um Elo spielen"** for a friendly that changes
   nobody's Elo. If exactly these teams met before, a hint shows how much the match still counts.
5. **"Match erstellen".** The table is now reserved for 10 minutes.

## 3. The other team agrees

The other team sees **"Aufstellung bestätigen"** on its home page (and on the table's QR page). One of them
opens the match and taps **"Ja, wir spielen mit"**. If the roster changes afterwards, it has to be confirmed
again. No phone at hand? A referee can record the agreement in person.

## 4. Play

- Anyone in the match taps **"Spiel starten"**. The stopwatch counts up for everyone; there is no time
  limit. After 20 minutes a gentle reminder appears ("Zeit fürs Finale"), nothing else happens.
- **"Pause"** / **"Weiterspielen"** stops and resumes the clock (for example while a cup is refilled).
- House rule: each team has exactly **2 throws per turn**, whatever its size. Solo players throw both balls,
  pairs one each, teams of three or four take turns. The usual rules (balls back, bounce, re-rack, last-cup
  redemption) apply.

## 5. Report and confirm the result

1. When the last cup falls, someone taps **"Match beenden"**, then the winning team, then checks the summary
   ("Stimmt das so?") and taps **"Resultat eintragen"**. Remaining cups are optional and never change Elo.
2. The table and all players are free immediately.
3. The other team sees the reported winner and taps **"Ja, bestätigen"**, or **"Nein, Einspruch"** if it is
   wrong (a referee then decides; until then the match does not count). The reporting team can still correct
   its report (**"Meldung korrigieren"**) as long as the other team has not answered.
4. After the confirmation both teams see the result with every person's Elo change and new rank.

## 6. How Elo works here

- Everyone starts the evening with **100 Elo**. A team's strength is the average of its players.
- Beating a stronger team brings more points than beating a weaker one. At most 24 points move per match,
  from the losing to the winning team, and they are split equally within each team. Playing solo means
  carrying the whole change alone: high risk, high reward.
- The same pairing (exactly the same people in the same two teams) counts fully twice, half the third time
  and not at all afterwards. Friendlies never count.
- With fewer than 3 rated games you are **"vorläufig"** (provisional) and cannot win the title yet. When the
  organizers close the round, the eligible player with the most Elo is champion (exact ties: co-champions).
- Achievements (most active, biggest upset, best comeback) are just for fun and never change Elo.

More detail: [rating system](rating-system.md).

## 7. Your profile

**"Profil"** in the menu shows your Elo, rank, wins and losses, best and lowest rating, a chart of your
progress (from 3 rated matches on, with the values as a table) and your recent matches, teammates and
opponents. Every name in the ranking opens that person's profile. E-mail addresses are never shown.
